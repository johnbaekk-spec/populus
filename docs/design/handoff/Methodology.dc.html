What our 13F derivations do — and refuse to do

· Amendments are typed: a RESTATEMENT supersedes; NEW-HOLDINGS merges. Originals are tombstoned, never deleted.
· Values are normalized across the 2023-01-03 unit change; every pre-change filing carries its unit basis.
· A change with an undisclosed side is n/c — never coerced into add or trim.
· "Exit" is a fact about the filing sequence, not a sale: delisting, confidential treatment, and filer migration all look identical in the record.
· Cross-filer rankings stop at the source's top-25 truncation, stated in place.
· Concentration is computed only when the denominator is fully disclosed; otherwise the stat prints "unavailable" with its reason concentration_unavailable.

Privacy — the reader is not the product

No tracking, no cookies, no fingerprinting, and no account required for anything on this site. Analytics are cookieless page counts (paths, referrers, coarse geography) — free-text search never leaves your device; searches resolve to page paths client-side. Watchlists live in your browser's localStorage and are never transmitted. If Populus ever offers paid conveniences (notifications, integrations), the records themselves stay free and ungated — a transparency site that paywalled the public record would contradict this page.
