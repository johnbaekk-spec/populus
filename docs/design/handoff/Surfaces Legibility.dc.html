Surfaces legibility — keep the tables, move the prose

The full-fat tables stay exactly as wide and as honest as they are today. What changes is where the explaining happens: definitions move into hover notes anchored on the thing they define, counts of what you are not seeing stay visible, and the page's first screen becomes a summary plus one deep link into the methodology. Four surfaces: /congress, /institutional, the individual member page and the individual filer page. Hover or click any this is the primitiveHover shows it. The text is in the DOM, is read by screen readers via aria-describedby, and is repeated in the “Notes on this table” block below every table — so it prints and survives with JavaScript off. below to try it.

Every number on this page is real, read from the live build 20260823.1 (code_sha b4787ff) on 2026-08-23: from /congress/data/feed.v1.json (71,632 txn rows, 72 date anomalies, 212 undated, and the measured 7d/30d/90d/12m window counts), from /congress/ and /institutional/, from the member page S001229 and from the filer page CIK 0002012383. How 13F data is made ↗·what our derivations refuse to do·amendment typing
What a 13F is — and is not Long-only · $100M+ managers · quarter-end snapshot · up to 45 days late · not a portfolio and not a census. Read the six structural limits ▾

13F reports cover LONG positions in Section 13(f) securities (US exchange-traded equities plus reportable options, warrants, certain convertibles) held by managers with at least $100M in such securities — so managers below that threshold are absent entirely. No short positions, no cash, no non-13(f) assets — not a full portfolio, and not a census of institutional ownership.

Positions are quarter-end snapshots filed up to 45 days late — not current holdings. A position may have been changed or closed before you ever see it, and is older still by however long ago that quarter ended.

Values are the manager's stated market value at quarter-end in era-dependent units (whole dollars for form versions effective 2023-01-03 onward, thousands before), normalized here to whole dollars with unit_basis carried on every record.

Affiliated managers may report the same position (otherManager), so one position can appear under more than one filer.

Positions under confidential treatment may be omitted until a later amendment discloses them.

These are disclosures, not investment advice (ARCHITECTURE.md §5.2 / M2-CONTRACT §5) · the full method ↗

8 · Individual filer page — and the worst offender on the site

The position-changes table renders a bare 32-character hash as its entire identity column. Live, on BlackRock (CIK 0002012383), for 5,532 quarter-over-quarter moves: sid:sec:prov:00076fbdb7a2ddaf78c0e89001ecf4f7 · exit · −$631M. Not one issuer name. The holdings table lower down the same page prints MICROSOFT CORP · COM · CUSIP 594918104 — so the name exists in data the page has already loaded.

The cause, and the fix. changesTableHtml takes QoqDeltaRow, which is selected from agg_qoq_deltas and has no issuer_name column (inst.ts:173). The filer's own reported rows do: serving_filer_rows carries (security_id, cusip, issuer_name, position_key) keyed by the same position_key (inst_serving.py:679). So this is a local join over data already on the page — no producer change. One trap: an exit is by definition absent from the current quarter's rows, so the join must run over the union of the previous and current period's rows for that filer. Joining only the current quarter would leave every exit — which is most of this table — unresolved. Where neither period has the name, the cell says so in words instead of printing the hash.

the rule, in one place

Hover-eligible — a definition. What a column means. How a number is computed. Why a column cannot be sorted. What a marker asserts. These do not change when the reader changes a control, and a reader who already knows them does not need them on screen.

Stays on the page — a live control state. “Show all 991 tickers (981 more)” and “4 rows disclose no amount for this column” both move when the reader moves a control, and both are the label of a control or a row inside the table. They are not prose about the data; they are the table describing its own current state.

Deleted from the page — said twice. The terminus row repeated the bound the expand button already states. The three exclusion counts moved into the window note. Nothing is unreachable; nothing is duplicated.

Three channels, one text. Hover · click-pin (touch and keyboard) · the print stylesheet. One source string renders all three, so they cannot drift.
