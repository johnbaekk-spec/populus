## M1 — the Congress tab, re-laid

M1 keeps the page's title and h1, "Congress signals" (the `LD9` reversal to "Signals" lands with the tabs in M2); its lede is "What stands out in Congress trades, each against the filer's own record." The triage is unchanged in value: `TRIAGE_VERSION` 1.0.0, the golden cases, tiers, meters, sentences and cards are as pinned, and every `getTriage()` row renders once. `signal-triage.ts` and `signals.v1.json` are untouched.

- **Tips (R1, LD3).** Every explanation on the page is an ⓘ: the `note()` primitive with the mark trigger. Above 720px it opens on hover, focus or click, anchored as before. At ≤720px an open note is a bottom sheet — `position: fixed`, inset 10px from the sides and bottom, at most 70vh, over a 55% scrim — through placement and top-layer rules only (`.note-pop` never gets `display:none`). It is one component, so the sheet applies wherever `note()` renders. Without JavaScript `popovertarget` opens it and a tap on the scrim light-dismisses it. With JavaScript, `notes.ts` stops anchoring the panel at that width (it clears the inline coordinates) and appends one `Close` button (`popovertargetaction="hide"`) on the first opening, from the script only, so the server markup and every parity capture are unchanged.
- **Rows (R2, R3, LD4).** A row is `article.si-row > details.si-row-d[name="si-rows"] > summary.si-row-sum`, plus the receipt (`.si-row-src`) as a sibling of the `<details>`, laid over the summary's reserved cell — no link is nested in the disclosure button. Line 1: ticker; the filer (party and state, the owner word, the LATE and grouping tags), one line in which only the name gives way to an ellipsis; range; the four meters; the filed date. Line 2: the side chip; the reason, cut to one line; "traded {date} · {lag}d"; a chevron. Below 1440px (the stacked band at 721–1080px and the split band's 1.5fr share up to 1439px) the meters move to line 2, between the reason and the trade date, so the row stays two lines at every width above the fold. Clicking the row, or Enter or Space on it, opens it in place with the full reason, the lead line's chronology, the grouping (a grouped row's latest filing labelled apart, D21), the members of a cluster, the owner, the LATE detail, every meter as a word with its definition (the Cmte line saying "context, never an allegation"), and links to the ticker page, the member page and the receipt. One row is open at a time (`details[name]`). The ticker page's On Signals block renders the same row opened, without the link to the page it is on.
- **Phones (R3).** At ≤720px a row is three lines: ticker, side, range, filed date and the receipt's cell; the filer, party and state, owner and tags beside the four meters, each labelled over its bars; the reason, the trade date and lag, and the chevron. A filer that needs more room wraps onto a further line rather than dropping a field.
- **Lead cards (R4, LD5).** v3's three cards, picks and CSS-only "Own record │ Absolute $" control are unchanged. Each pick carries `a.si-card-open` to its row (`#si-row-{slug(key)}`), stretched under the card's own ticker, member and receipt links; the client opens the row on the click and on `hashchange` (and for a hash on load), selecting the Filings pane and opening Routine first when the row needs them. The client shows it, and puts `hidden` on the unselected pane, only while `(max-width: 1080px)` matches; no media rule hides a pane. A hash naming an element in a hidden pane (`#hits`, `#families`, a row) selects that pane first, so no deep link lands on a hidden pane (step 2 of M2's `revealTarget`). ### Implementation readings recorded against the plan

- **The page budget at production scale is modelled, not measured, and the model fits it at 30 a slice.** This build's registry types no filer, so it holds no move (below); the 1,572,864-byte budget passes here at 306,657 bytes. The size model (`.codex-review/m2/realistic.ts`, outside the tree) is built from the live distributions of build 20261016.3 (fetched 2026-10-17): the 2,565 published new and exit rows of `notable-moves/2026-03-31.v1.json` as they are (36 of the 37 notable managers, their types, issuers, tickers, receipts and keys), the 50 live Consensus rows (New + Add + Trim + Exit = 384, at most 25 on one name), and the remaining 7,688 changes as adds and trims by the same managers in the shard's type mix (split 4,075 / 3,613, the board's own 96 : 85), on issuers the board does not list; the Congress lines are this build's. Its board shares 45 of the live 50 names and carries 364 changes on them. Its DOM set is **864 rows at 30 a slice and 720 at 20**. Before the row was slimmed (next reading) a row averaged 1,564 bytes and the page would have been 1,808,091 bytes at 30 and 1,585,681 at 20 — over the budget at both; after it, a row averages 1,201 bytes and the page is **1,494,610 bytes at 30** (78,254 under the budget) and **1,323,706 at 20**. The margin at 30 is thin, so the page applies LD11's fallback itself (below); the post-build budget test does NOT stop a publish — `publish.yml` runs `npm run test:post` report-only (A4-02: a failure is a warning, never a failed publish) — so the page's own fallback is what keeps an over-budget body from shipping, and a page still over the budget at 20 would ship with a warning.
- **A held move row carries its data, not its opened body (LD11's byte budget).** The first `NAMED_MOVES_MIN` rows — all a reader without script can show and open — carry their opened body (`moveDetailHtml`). A held row (`hidden`) carries an empty body and the data it is rendered from: `data-fp` (the filer page), `data-tv` (its verification date, only where it is not the section's `data-mv-tv`) and `data-cg` (its counted Congress members, only when some are), beside what its summary and its section already say (type, kind, filed date, ticker, `data-mv-period`, `data-mv-q`). The client renders the body on the row's first opening with the same `moveDetailHtml`, so the two cannot differ (a unit test compares every held row's client body with the server's body for the same move, byte for byte). A held row whose side only broad accounts traded keeps its server body, since that statement names the accounts. The collapsed row is unchanged in what it says; its kind word is one span (`.mv-k.qoq-chip`), its receipt is the `a.mv-src` itself, and its Congress words name the column and leave the quarter to the column's tip ("Congress: none on this side", "Congress: 2 members with a purchase"; a name with no reviewed ticker still says "no reviewed ticker, so no Congress match"). On the fixture a row went from 1,532 to 1,092 bytes on average (a held row 1,533 → 1,061; the 15 full rows 1,513 → 1,465); on the size model from 1,564 to 1,201. A target of about 900 bytes a row was not reached: the rest is the collapsed row's own fields — a 13F receipt URL of about 85 characters, the filer page, the four words assistive technology needs (the column head is `aria-hidden`), `<time datetime>` and the site's class hooks — none of which this change drops.
- **Row markup (LD11).** `data-kind`, `data-type` and `data-ikey` sit on the `article.mv-item` wrapper that also holds the sibling receipt, not on the `<details>`; there is no `data-tk` — the client reads a row's ticker from its `.mv-tk`. The ticker's verification is a line of the opened row ("NVDA — verified against the SEC company list on {date}"), not a note inside the summary, which holds no interactive content.
- **The Congress side (LD10) renders once per ticker and side.** Rendering each side's lines (up to 12) inside every move row repeats the same lines in every row on that ticker. The page carries them once, in a closed "Congress lines behind the counts" disclosure at the Named moves' foot; an opened row, and a name's Consensus detail, shows its side's count with a link to them, and with script the side's lines are copied into the opened row on its first opening. Without script they are one link away.
- **The Consensus detail (LD10) is the name filter.** Choosing a Consensus row (or a card) filters the Named moves to its issuer key and shows that name's Congress lines, both sides, above the list (`.mv-name-detail`).
- **`data-mv-totals`** is a quote-free list of `kind|type:count` pairs rather than a JSON string. It carries the same 30 numbers and needs no entity decoding to be read back (the unit-test DOM, like any careless reader, does not decode entities in attributes).
- **The page applies LD11's fallback itself.** `signals/index.astro` renders the body at 30 a slice and, when the body is above the budget less a 64 KiB allowance for the layout's chrome (`SIGNALS_PAGE_CHROME_BYTES`), renders it again at `NAMED_MOVES_FALLBACK_ROWS` (20). The post-build test holds the built file to 1,572,864 bytes, but it is report-only in the publish workflow, so this fallback is the mechanism that keeps the page within the budget. On this build the fallback did not apply.
- **Consensus layout (LD12).** Line 1 has seven columns — ticker, issuer, new, add, trim, exit, net $ — to fit the 1fr side column; the Congress count sits on line 2 under net $, beside the top mover, and the column head's "Cong" sits under "Net $" the same way.
- **The Consensus card's count** reads "{n} filings — see the moves" with n the distinct notable managers in the name (one 13F each for the quarter), not the four kind counts summed, which count a manager with two changed positions in the name twice.
- **Tab counts** reuse the ticker page's `.tk-tab-n`. **"Top add"** labels the Institutional ledger's Consensus figure (`consensusAddLedgerItem`), which names the issuer and how many notable managers opened it.
- **Hashes.** `selectTabFor` writes `#{panel}` (`history.replaceState`) only when the current hash does not already name an element inside that panel, so a deep link keeps its own hash.
- **Scroll regions.** `initScrollRegions` observes a region's content (its table, else its first element child) for size changes, since the tab strip's wrapper holds a tablist, not a table.
- **Fold gate.** `css-fold.test.ts`'s `ruleAppliesTo` skips pseudo-element selectors (a `::after` caret matches no element, so it read as a dropped honesty element); a control pins it. Chip rows that must not wrap on a phone scroll as a grid of `max-content` columns: `white-space: nowrap` on `.chips` is a fold-gate violation.
- **Geometry without a ledger route.** This build has no move, so the Institutional tab's geometry (`signals-v4.spec.ts`) runs on the shared fixture (`test/lib/inst-signals-fixture.ts`) spliced into the built page (the fit test adds, opt-in, fourteen keyed names so its Consensus has a "Show all"); `ledger.spec.ts`'s `/institutional/` route now expects no pair, one table and five columns (the filer directory) now that its bands have left. Three older specs were scoped to what they measure: `design-reference.spec.ts` reads the Congress panel's pane switch (the Institutional tab has its own), the print test in `sl-notes.spec.ts` reads the mark tips inside table cells (the reduced `/institutional/` head carries tips beside its heading and chips), and the Top add control in `ledger.spec.ts` uses a 41-character name, because the Signals head's unmarked figure is 166px at 1440 and 171px at 390 (measured), where the 26- and 28-character subs already fit two lines.
- **Deleted helpers.** `classifyNotableMovesShard` and `notableMovesHref` had no consumer once the band left; the shard route itself is unchanged (`R23`).
- **Testability.** `initSignalsPage({ tabsEnv })` takes the tab binder's environment from a test; the page passes none.
